# Mechanics

Key equations for constant acceleration:

| Equation | Missing variable |
|---|---|
| v = v₀ + a·t | distance |
| d = v₀·t + ½·a·t² | final velocity |
| v² = v₀² + 2·a·d | time |

---

## Problem 1: accelerating car

A car starts from rest and accelerates at 3.0 m/s² for 5.0 s. Find its final speed and the distance traveled.

- **Given:** v₀ = 0, a = 3.0 m/s², t = 5.0 s
- **Speed:** v = 0 + (3.0)(5.0) = **15 m/s**
- **Distance:** d = 0 + ½(3.0)(5.0)² = ½(3.0)(25) = **37.5 m**
- **Check:** average speed = (0 + 15)/2 = 7.5 m/s; 7.5 m/s × 5.0 s = 37.5 m ✓

## Problem 2: free fall

A ball is dropped from a height of 20 m. How long does it take to reach the ground, and how fast is it moving just before impact?

- **Given:** v₀ = 0, d = 20 m, a = g = 9.8 m/s²
- **Time:** 20 = ½(9.8)t² → t² = 40/9.8 = 4.08 → t = **2.02 s**
- **Speed:** v = g·t = 9.8 × 2.02 = **19.8 m/s**
- **Check (second method):** v = √(2gd) = √(2 × 9.8 × 20) = √392 = 19.8 m/s ✓

## Problem 3: horizontal projectile

A ball is thrown horizontally at 10 m/s from a ledge 4.9 m high. How far from the base of the ledge does it land?

- Horizontal and vertical motion are independent.
- **Vertical (time to fall):** 4.9 = ½(9.8)t² → t² = 1.0 → t = **1.0 s**
- **Horizontal (constant speed):** x = 10 m/s × 1.0 s = **10 m**
- **Common error:** using the 10 m/s horizontal speed to compute fall time. The horizontal speed does not change how long the ball takes to fall.

## Problem 4: Newton's second law with friction

A 10 kg box is pushed across the floor with a horizontal force of 50 N. Kinetic friction on the box is 20 N. Find the acceleration.

- **Net force:** F_net = 50 N − 20 N = 30 N (in the direction of the push)
- **Acceleration:** a = F_net / m = 30 / 10 = **3.0 m/s²**
- **Common error:** dividing the applied force (50 N) by mass and forgetting friction, giving 5.0 m/s².

## Problem 5: mass vs weight

A person has a mass of 60 kg. What is their weight on Earth and on the Moon (g_Moon ≈ 1.62 m/s²)?

- **Earth:** W = m·g = 60 × 9.8 = **588 N**
- **Moon:** W = 60 × 1.62 = **97.2 N**
- **Mass** is **60 kg in both places**; only weight changes.

## Problem 6: conservation of energy

A 2.0 kg ball is released from rest 5.0 m above the ground. Find its potential energy at the top and its speed just before hitting the ground.

- **Potential energy:** PE = m·g·h = 2.0 × 9.8 × 5.0 = **98 J**
- **At the bottom**, all PE has become kinetic energy: ½·m·v² = 98 J → v² = 98 J / (½ × 2.0 kg) = 98 m²/s² → v = **9.90 m/s**
- **Check:** v = √(2gh) = √(2 × 9.8 × 5.0) = √98 = 9.90 m/s ✓. The speed does not depend on mass.
